## 2. Content Ratio Formula (Anti-Info-Dump Rule)

People on TikTok/Reels swipe past plain price tables in 1.5 seconds. Follow the **70 / 20 / 10** feed mix:

- **70% Relatable Travel Pain & Survival Hacks:** Specific airport, train, map, or payment panics every Thai traveler fears (Visit Japan Web freezing at Narita gate, Google Maps showing only straight lines in Seoul, Alipay/LINE failing in Shanghai, Suica top-up needing an active connection).
- **20% Pain → eSIM Setup & Prevention Bridge:** Short visual fixes (`*#06#` EID check, turning OFF iPhone `Allow Cellular Data Switching` so Thai SIM doesn't trigger shock roaming bills, installing China eSIM before boarding).
- **10% Direct Budget Comparison & Promo Code:** Concise side-by-side money savers (`Airalo 5GB/7 วัน ฿350` → `~฿298` with `NEWTOAIRALO15` vs `฿399` travel SIM cards) linking straight to comparison tables.
